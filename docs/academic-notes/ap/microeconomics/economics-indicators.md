# Economics Indicators

## The Circular Flow & GDP

$GDP\ Deflator = \dfrac{Nominal\ GDP}{Real\ GDP} \times 100$

## Unemployment

Three Types of Adults:

1. Employed
2. Unemployed
3. Not in the labor force

$Labor\ force = Employed + Unemployed$

$Unemployment\ rate = \dfrac{Number\ of\ unemployed}{Labor\ force} \times 100\%$

$Labor-force\ participation\ rate=\dfrac{Labor\ force}{Adult\ population} \times 100\%$

What is wrong with the unemployment rate:

- Discouraged workers
	- Nonworking people who **are** capable of working but have given up looking for a job due to the state of the job market
- Part-time workers
- Illegal labor

Three Types of Unemployment:

- Frictional unemployment
	- Unemployment due to the time workers spend in job search
	- Temporarily, being between jobs
	- Qualified workers with transferable skills but aren't working
	- Exists even when *number of job seekers = number of jobs offered*
	- Does **not** mean there is a surplus of labor
- Structural unemployment
	- Unemployment that results when *number of job seekers > number of jobs offered*
	- Can be caused by
		- Technology progress
		- Change in the economic structure
		- Binding minimum wages
		- The actions of **labor unions** can have effects on minimum wages
		- The use of **efficiency wages** by firms
- Cyclical unemployment
	- Unemployment that results from **economic recessions**
	- As demand for G&S falls, **demand for labor** falls
	- When economic condition improved, cyclical unemployment will be reduced

**Frictional unemployment** and **Structural unemployment** are **unavoidable**

$Natural\ unemployment = Frictional\ unemployment + Structural\ unemployment$

$Actual\ unemployment = Natural\ unemployment + Cyclical\ unemployment$

**Full employment** is achieved if we have **only** natural rate of unemployment, i.e. **No Cyclical Unemployment**.
