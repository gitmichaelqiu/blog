## Two-Way Tables & Relative Frequencies

![](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto,width=2048/https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2024/09/50640_frequency-two-way-table.png)

### Relative Frequencies

- Joint relative frequencies: $\frac{joint\ frequency}{total\ frequency}$
- Marginal Relative Frequencies: $\frac{marginal\ frequency}{total\ frequency}$

![](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto,width=2048/https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2024/09/13726_joint-and-marginal-relative-frequency.png)

### Conditional Relative Frequencies

Conditional Relative Frequencies =$\dfrac{joint\ frequency}{marginal\ frequency}$

Conditional of category X: the row/column of category sum up to 1

![](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto,width=2048/https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2024/09/57722_conditional-relative-frequencies.png)

### Mosaic Plots

![](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto,width=2048/https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2024/09/41858_mosaic-plot.png)
- **Widths** are **proportional** to the **marginal relative frequencies**
- **Areas** of the bars are **proportional** to the **joint frequencies**
- Compare **conditional relative frequencies**
- Compare **marginal relative frequencies** by checking **widths** of the bars
