## Ollama Writing Tools

[![License: Apache-2.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-Apache%202.0-blue.svg)](https://github.com/gitmichaelqiu/OllamaWritingTools/blob/main/LICENSE)
[![Powered by Ollama](https://img.shields.io/badge/Powered%20by-Ollama-orange)](https://ollama.com)
[![Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/gitmichaelqiu/OllamaWritingTools?color=green)](https://github.com/gitmichaelqiu/OllamaWritingTools/releases/)

[Ollama Writing Tools](https://github.com/gitmichaelqiu/OllamaWritingTools), is a **Windows** application that enhances your writing with AI-powered assistance through Ollama. **Simply select text anywhere**, and get instant writing improvements with a single click. It is 100% **free and open source**, and even **customizable**, you can configure different prompts for different writing tasks.

## (Raycast Extension) Lyric Fever Control

[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-blue.svg)](https://github.com/gitmichaelqiu/Raycast-LyricFeverControl/blob/main/LICENSE)
